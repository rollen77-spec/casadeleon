Discover the tranquil elegance of Casa de Leon Studio

Unmatched comfort and modern luxury

Casa de Leon offers a serene retreat designed with your relaxation in mind. Located in Lakeview, Mississauga, the spacious and modern studio is bathed in natural light thanks to its large windows and accordion patio doors. These doors open seamlessly to a beautifully landscaped backyard oasis, creating the perfect blend of indoor and outdoor living. Guests can enjoy exclusive amenities such as a private basketball court, a large deck equipped with a BBQ, a 4K projector with Google Chromecast for streaming, a wine fridge, an espresso bar, and a full bathroom featuring heated floors for added comfort.

Prime location in a family-friendly neighbourhood

Situated in a quiet and welcoming community, Casa de Leon offers convenient access to major highways, Toronto Pearson Airport just 11 minutes away, and downtown Toronto within a 15-minute drive. This prime location is ideal for both leisure and business travellers looking to explore the Greater Toronto Area. Nearby shopping destinations like Applewood Plaza, Sherway Gardens, and Dixie Outlet Mall provide ample opportunities for retail therapy, while scenic walking trails in Port Credit and along the Humber River offer peaceful escapes into nature.

Exclusivity and safety for a premium guest experience

Casa de Leon is dedicated to providing a safe and exclusive environment for its guests. To maintain this standard, guests must be 25 years or older, present valid identification, and sign a waiver agreement upon booking. This policy ensures a mature, respectful atmosphere where visitors can fully unwind and enjoy all the luxurious amenities. Whether you're seeking a weekend getaway or a longer stay, Casa de Leon promises a unique blend of comfort, convenience, and tranquility that sets it apart from typical accommodations.
